Machine Learning course · ADA University · 2026

Ensemble Learning with Decision Trees

Can a thousand small trees out-vote one carefully tuned tree?

The majority vote reaches 87.3% test accuracy, higher than the tuned single tree (85.95%) and the average small tree (80.6%).

How I did it

I tuned a decision tree classifier with GridSearchCV, using 3-fold cross-validation over max depth, max leaf nodes and min samples split, on a 10,000-sample, two-class dataset.

Then I built a random-forest-style ensemble from scratch: 1,000 trees, each trained on a random 100-sample subset (ShuffleSplit) and combined by majority vote.

Machine Learning course · ADA University · 2026

Linear & Logistic Regression from Scratch

Can I match scikit-learn with nothing but NumPy?

The linear model reaches R² ≈ 0.79, confirmed against scikit-learn's LinearRegression and a NumPy normal-equation solution. The logistic model reaches 89% accuracy, matching scikit-learn.

How I did it

I implemented linear regression with NumPy alone (hypothesis, cost function, gradient descent) to predict car prices from mileage and model year on Turbo.az listings, after cleaning string and multi-currency data and applying z-score normalization.

I then built logistic regression from scratch (sigmoid, cross-entropy cost, reusable gradient descent) with min-max scaling to predict whether a student passes or fails, and plotted the fits, cost curves and decision boundary with Matplotlib.

ADA University · 2026

Store Performance Analysis

Where does a store actually make its money, and where does it lose it?

Orders discounted above 20% lose about $97 each on average, while orders with no discount earn about $67. My recommendation: limit heavy discounting.

How I did it

I analyzed 9,994 orders (about $2.3M in revenue, 2014–2017) from the Superstore dataset with pandas and NumPy, answering business questions on sales, profit, regions, customer segments and discounting, then summarized the findings for a store manager.

  • Technology drives the most revenue and profit, while Tables, Bookcases and Supplies lose money.
  • The Central region has the highest average discount (24%) and the lowest profit margin (about 8%).
  • NumPy z-scores flag 247 outlier orders (2.47%).
